01 / THE FINDING

The strongest asset is not any single homepage. It is the relationship between them.

The collection already behaves like a multiverse: ichoTaKu is the identity spine; Afterdark and the moving discography are playable media worlds; the district pages turn values into places; CHOTAKU is the production engine; DigitalDive is the interpretive layer.

The next level is a provenance-aware system that can compare variants, extract reusable design DNA, connect songs and characters, and recommend what to publish—while preserving every source.

03 / DESIGN DNA

A coherent language is already emerging.

The pages diverge in purpose, but repeat enough visual and narrative motifs to form a system.

A

Signal typography

Oversized condensed headlines act like broadcast identifiers. They make each district legible from a distance and give the ecosystem a poster-like rhythm.

B

Night-spectrum palette

Near-black grounds, electric magenta, cyan, bone white, and occasional acid accents create continuity across creator, music, archive, and systems surfaces.

C

Worlds as navigation

Archive, radio, alley, labs, camp, systems, and builds are not generic menu labels. They are narrative places that explain function through atmosphere.

D

Evidence over polish

Build journals, previews, catalog exports, and alternate versions expose the making process. That raw provenance is a differentiator—not clutter to erase.

04 / MODEL & PROVIDER ROUTING

Use a relay team, not one “magic” model.

Each provider should own the layer it is best suited to, while the manifest and source files remain the authority.

SITESPresentation + private deployment

Turns the local evidence into a durable, navigable experience. Best for the atlas UI, private publishing, and future scheduled refreshes.

ROLE / EXPERIENCE LAYER
OPENAICross-page reasoning

Use multimodal reasoning for taxonomy, contradiction checks, structured comparisons, narrative synthesis, and tool-orchestrated updates.

ROLE / ORCHESTRATOR + SYNTHESIS
CLAUDE DESIGNEditorial and frontend critique

Use as a second design voice: challenge hierarchy, identify generic patterns, propose alternate compositions, and review long-form creative direction.

ROLE / ADVERSARIAL DESIGN REVIEW
GEMINIHigh-volume visual inspection

Strong fit for batch image classification, text extraction, visual Q&A, and structured tagging across dozens or hundreds of screenshots.

ROLE / MULTIMODAL BATCH ANALYST
OLLAMAPrivate local preprocessing

Use for local captions, palette extraction, similarity proposals, and sensitive metadata. Ollama is present locally, but no models are installed yet.

ROLE / PRIVATE BULK WORKER · NOT YET PROVISIONED
HUMANMeaning and release authority

Models can identify patterns; they cannot decide which version carries the right history, emotional truth, or release intent. Those remain creator decisions.
